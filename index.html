Second Thought

We go through life taking so much for granted, often simply because we’ve never had the time to stop, ask questions, and understand the things around us. And even when curiosity strikes, it’s not always clear where to turn or who can help us find the answers.

In my experience, especially in the corporate world, where speed and delivery are so highly valued, taking a step back to ask questions can feel like a luxury.

AI has become the perfect thought partner: a second mind to bounce ideas off, challenge assumptions, and help me see things from a different perspective. It’s not just about helping me work faster or more effectively. It’s also given me the freedom to indulge my curiosity beyond work, finally exploring the questions I’ve always wondered about but never had the time, resources, or right person to ask.

If you enjoy exploring the world around you, going down rabbit holes, and learning about things you never knew you wanted to know, come join me on Substack.
